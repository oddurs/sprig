## Purpose

The launch itself, as an item with its own acceptance criteria.

## Approach

Post the Show HN with the title and first comment from the site plan, on the first Tuesday after the gates pass, around 08:30 US Eastern. Stay in the thread for six hours and file real criticism as items the same day.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The post and the first comment are up, with no attribution to any tool.
- [ ] Every substantive criticism from the thread is an item within a day.
