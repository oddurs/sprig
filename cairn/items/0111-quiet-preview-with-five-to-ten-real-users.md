## Purpose

The first hour decides a Show HN, and a broken install on a platform nobody tested fills it.

## Approach

A week before the launch, ask five to ten people who would use Sprig on a real plan to try the site and the binaries, and report problems. Nobody is asked to vote or comment on the day.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At least five people tried it on a real plan, and every problem they found is an item.
- [ ] Nobody was asked for votes or launch-day comments.
