## Context

Every other mark is punctuation, and a note starting with 'x ' becomes an item.

## Options and tradeoffs

A symbol for consistency, or `x` because people tick paper that way and todo.txt proved it in plain text.

## Decision

`x` stays. A note that really starts with 'x ' is escaped with a backslash.

## Revisit when

Never.
