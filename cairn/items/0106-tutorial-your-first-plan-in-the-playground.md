## Reader and question

Someone who has never seen Sprig and wants to write a real plan in five minutes, before any tool is installed.

## Change

A tutorial that takes the reader through marks, nesting, a dependency, a question and its answer, and a second file, all in the playground, with each step's result visible.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every snippet in the tutorial is a valid Sprig file.
- [ ] The tutorial names v0.1 as the release that brings the same steps to the terminal, without describing commands that don't exist yet.
